Medicare Key Enrollment Windows

Know when you can enroll or switch

IEP
Initial Enrollment Period
7-month window around your 65th birthday (3 months before, month of, 3 months after).
AEP
Annual Enrollment Period (Oct 15 - Dec 7)
The yearly period where anyone with Medicare can join, switch, or drop Medicare Advantage & Part D plans.
OEP
Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment (Jan 1 - Mar 31)
Switch between Advantage plans or return to Original Medicare with a Part D drug plan.

Carriers offer multi-policy discounts when you purchase both auto and homeowners or renters insurance through the same provider. By placing both policies together, discounts typically range between 15% to 25% across your total premium.
